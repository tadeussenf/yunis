Sharing the Sound

Music is not taught; it is uncovered. Whether you are holding a wooden acoustic guitar, a mesmerizing handpan, or a simple wooden flute, the goal is not perfection, but pure expression.

In these personalized 1-on-1 sessions, I will guide you to intuitively connect with your instrument. We focus less on rigid theory and more on opening the ear, trusting your hands, and breathing with the rhythm of the strings and steel. Let's explore the healing power of creating your own medicine through sound.
